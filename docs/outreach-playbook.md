## How to use it

- Replace `[case-study-url]` with `https://crisiscore-systems.ca/case-study/pain-tracker` unless you have a stronger route for that audience.
- For dev.to, Hashnode, and profile links, replace the plain URL with the matching tagged version from the campaign-tagged URLs section.
- Replace `[product/company]` only when you have a real reason to personalize.
- Keep the first message short. The page should do the heavy lifting.
- Default to one link only: the case study for proof, or the trust-risk-read page for conversion.
- Only add the homepage, proof page, or Protective Computing canon if the recipient asks for the broader surface or deeper theory.

## What to watch

- Replies that ask about scope, timing, or fit are positive signals.
- Silence after a click usually means the case study did not create enough urgency or fit clarity.
- Clicks with no contact action suggest the page may need a sharper next move.
- Repeated questions about pricing or engagement shape suggest the front-door offer still needs tighter qualification.
